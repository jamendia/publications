# The *soler* + INF periphrasis in Spanish

## Citation 

**Mendia, J.A.** 2025. "The *soler* + INF periphrasis in Spanish."
*Proceedings of the 41st West Coast Conference on Formal Linguistics* 41: 442-447.

[Book website](https://www.lingref.com/cpp/wccfl/41/).


## Abstract

This short paper describes the main syntactic, semantic and pragmatic properties of habitual constructions with *soler* and sketches a first corresponding formal analysis capturing its main semantic properties, its differences with restpect to habitual readings of its unmarked imperfective counterpart, and the pragmatic inferences that soler is argued to convey.
